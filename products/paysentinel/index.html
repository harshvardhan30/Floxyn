    FAQ

    Questions about PaySentinel

    Further reading:

    Rules vs machine learning for payment fraud
    What data does PaySentinel need?

    Your historical transaction records with outcomes (for example chargebacks or confirmed fraud), plus the fields you already capture such as device, merchant, amount and time.

    Does it replace our existing rules?

    It doesn’t have to. Many teams run it alongside their rules first, compare decisions, then decide how to combine them.

    Where does it run?

    In your environment or a cloud account you approve. Transaction data doesn’t need to leave your control.

    What happens when fraud patterns change?

    Drift monitoring flags when incoming data or model performance shifts, so you can retrain, or roll back to a previous model version.
