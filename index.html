About Auto Solution

A remote-first automation and analytics studio with a simple mission: give people back the hours they lose to repetitive, manual work.

Auto Solution started when our founders kept watching skilled teams burn entire days on copy-paste reporting, manual reconciliation and Excel busywork — tasks a script could finish in seconds. So we built one. Then another. Then a studio around it.

Today we design and ship Python, Excel, OCR and ML-powered automation for businesses across India, the Middle East, Singapore and beyond — a senior, remote-first team with no bloated process.

We measure ourselves the way our clients do: hours saved, errors removed, and how fast a project goes from "manual mess" to "it just runs now."
