    GST reconciliation, every month

    Tally to Python to Excel, fully automated.

    CA firm, India
    Read how we did it
    The problem
    Each month the team exported data from Tally and reconciled it against GST records by hand in Excel, about three hours per report.
    What we built
    A Python pipeline that pulls straight from Tally, matches every entry, flags mismatches for review and produces GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B drafts in Excel.
    The result
    Under five minutes per report, with people only looking at the handful of entries that actually need judgement.
