Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information the Auto Solution website (autosolution.com) handles and why. It covers this website only; client projects are governed by the NDA and contract signed for each engagement.

What we collect

  • Contact form and checklist requests. These don't send anything to our servers. Submitting opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled, and nothing reaches us until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.
  • Email and phone. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to share.

What is stored on your device

The site stores a few small preferences in your browser's local storage: your light/dark theme choice, your response to the cookie notice, and (for the current browser session only) whether you've already seen our audit offer. These never leave your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) handles any chat you start from the site.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and reliability.

How we use your information

Only to reply to your enquiry, scope and deliver work you ask for, and keep in touch about that work. We do not sell or rent personal data.

Your choices

You can clear the stored preferences at any time through your browser settings. To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, contact us at info@autosoluation.com or on WhatsApp at +91 73038 97496.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
